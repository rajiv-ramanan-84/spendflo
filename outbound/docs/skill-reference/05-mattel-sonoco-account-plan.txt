Mattel and Sonoco outbound sales plans

LinkedIn email and callingPrepared for Siddharth and the outbound team30 October 2026

This document contains two account plans. Mattel focuses on supplier redlining within Coupa; Sonoco tests whether software renewal preparation remains manual around its Coupa and Fairmarkit setup. Each plan includes targeting, coordinated outreach, reply handling and qualification. The Mattel plan follows below; the Sonoco plan begins on page 8.

The proposition

Spendflo is implementing a workflow for a Coupa customer that picks up supplier paper during contract approval, redlines it against the customer’s templates and policies, and returns the marked-up document and change summary to the contract in Coupa. Legal retains review.

What makes this relevant

Mattel’s public procurement role descriptions connect supplier contracting with Legal, and recent Coupa role descriptions support an operational Coupa environment. These are fit indicators. Contract backlog, review delays and demand for automation still need to be established in conversation.

Who owns execution

Owner | Responsibility

Siddharth | Send the senior email and LinkedIn outreach; lead engaged buyer conversations.

SDR or researcher | Verify current contacts, make calls, log outcomes and coordinate follow-up.

Product or engineering | Prepare the sample, verify supported workflow and answer integration questions.



Before the first touch

Identify a current procurement leader and one operational contract owner. Check for a genuine warm introduction. Prepare a shareable sample using synthetic or approved material. Confirm which parts of the workflow can be demonstrated today. Use “a Coupa customer” unless Datavant has approved external naming.

Relevant legal stakeholder | Defines review policies, exceptions and human decisions. | Include after procurement confirms relevance.



Use the signals accurately

Coupa use is strongly supported by employer role descriptions. Recent Hyderabad Coupa hiring is visible in indexed sources, but an individual job redirects, so the vacancy’s current status is uncertain. Say “recent Coupa hiring,” if needed; avoid making hiring the central pitch.

Procurement role descriptions include supplier negotiations and contract review with Legal. An employer logistics procurement listing now explicitly says it has expired. Use it as workflow evidence, not an active hiring trigger.

Company-wide AI adoption and the supply-chain savings program are background context. Neither establishes a procurement AI budget, a legal bottleneck or a hiring freeze. Re-sourcing was announced in 2025; the 500-SKU figure was for that year, not an annual run rate.

Claims to leave out

Do not assert that Mattel has no contract AI, no other CLM or intake tool, an overloaded legal team, or a confirmed contract backlog. Do not promise a Mattel-specific integration, savings percentage, implementation duration or pricing before validation.

Use the Datavant work as implementation evidence

The supplied SOW targets production go-live on 16 November 2026. Describe it as an implementation underway until delivery is confirmed. Do not present its scope as achieved results. The attachment selection, approval-step behaviour and contract write permissions must be verified before making specific commitments.



Cadence and account coordination

Use business days from launch and the recipient’s local working hours. One account owner coordinates all channels. The sequence below is a starting experiment; a reply or referral changes the plan immediately.

Day | Channel | Action

1 | Email | Siddharth sends the approved opening to the procurement leader.

2 | Call | SDR validates ownership and offers the sample. Leave one brief voicemail if appropriate.

4 | LinkedIn | Siddharth sends a contextual connection request.

6 | Email | Follow up with what the sample lets the buyer assess.

8 | Call | Second attempt. Ask about relevance or the correct owner.

10 | Email | If still unengaged, approach one operational owner with the secondary email. Do not send another senior email that day.

12 | LinkedIn | If connected and unengaged, ask whether to send the sample. Skip if not connected.

15 | Email | Close the loop with the primary contact. End the active sequence if unanswered.



Rules for coordinated follow-up

Pause scheduled touches when anyone replies. Respect opt-outs and explicit disinterest. Do not run the full sequence against every Mattel contact. If an operational owner engages, ask how to involve the leader rather than continuing parallel cold touches.

If a sample is requested, send it promptly and switch to the response path on page 6. Do not require a discovery call to access the sample. If the sample is not ready, finish it before sending the offer.

What to record

Track the verified contact and role, channel, touch date, reply, referral, identified workflow, existing automation, next action, owner and due date. Record buyer language verbatim where useful. Email opens are not a reason to intensify outreach.

Handle the likely replies

Already use Coupa or contract AI: “Understood. Does it already cover policy-based first-pass review and return the result into your approval workflow? If so, this may already be covered.”

Use ChatGPT: “Which parts of supplier review does it cover today? The workflow we’re implementing connects the review output to the Coupa contract and Legal’s next step.”

Ask about integration or security: capture the specific question and involve product or engineering. Do not assume Mattel’s configuration matches Datavant’s or improvise technical commitments.

Interested but later: establish a relevant timing event and agreed follow-up date. No interest or opt-out: stop. No meaningful workflow gap: close the account rather than manufacturing a project.

The first meeting and success criteria

Use a 20-minute working session to identify the agreement type, monthly volume, current review owner, approximate turnaround time, existing tools and the work still performed manually. Establish who owns improvement and whether there is a near-term reason to act. Bring product or engineering for technical validation.

Advance to a scoped proof only when the problem, owner and supported workflow are clear. Agree the baseline, evaluation criteria, customer inputs, human checkpoints, delivery owners and decision date. Measure review quality and actual work removed; do not promise a percentage improvement without evidence.

Campaign success is a useful reply followed by a held conversation that confirms a consequential problem, an owner and an agreed next step. Track delivered emails, positive replies, sample requests, meetings held and qualified opportunities separately. A sample request alone is not a qualified opportunity.

Sonoco GTM plan

Lead with one job to validate: preparing software renewals for an owner decision before the notice deadline. Position Flo around the work still performed manually, if any, within Sonoco’s established procurement automation environment. Do not assume that Coupa or Fairmarkit leaves this work uncovered.

The proposed job

Assemble the contract terms, cancellation deadline, accountable owner, supplier context and pricing benchmark; get the owner’s decision in time to act; then initiate a prefilled renewal request in Coupa where supported. This reflects the Datavant implementation scope, rather than proof that Sonoco needs the same workflow.

Why this account is worth investigating

Sonoco confirms Coupa for indirect purchase orders and supplier onboarding. Fairmarkit documents a Coupa-integrated request-to-sourcing workflow. Sonoco’s October 2026 results cite continued procurement savings and cost pressure. These establish fit and an existing investment in automation; they do not establish missed notices or renewal leakage.

Prioritize the possible gaps

Priority | Job to test | Disqualifying answer

Second | Commercial questions and policy exceptions across contracts. | An existing contract tool answers those questions adequately.

Third | Contract review and the manual contract-to-PR step. | No material manual workload or existing automation covers it.

When the buyer confirms manual work

Ask which part takes the effort: finding the terms, obtaining the owner’s decision, or starting the renewal. Then establish the consequence: late decisions, lost negotiation time or administrative effort. Ask who owns improving it. Avoid assuming missed notices from manual work alone.

The next step

Offer a short walkthrough of the exact step identified. Use synthetic or approved material and show notice terms linked to their source, owner context, the decision and the resulting request. Clearly distinguish a prepared demonstration from a production result. Product should verify available scope before the walkthrough is promised.

Advance or close

Advance when there is a material task, a consequence, an owner and a supported way to remove work. Before a proof of value, agree a baseline, required data, human decisions, success criteria and a decision date. If renewals are covered, ask whether cross-contract commercial questions remain difficult; do not automatically switch to another pitch.

Ownership and measurement

Siddharth owns senior outreach and commercial follow-up; the SDR owns contact verification, calls and account notes; product owns capability and integration validation. Track positive replies, verified owners, confirmed manual steps, walkthroughs held and qualified opportunities. Record “already covered” as a useful disqualification rather than a messaging failure.
